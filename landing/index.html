How it works

The AI does the banker's judgment work. PayPal moves the money.

Every hand is a PayPal invoice

Members pay in PayPal, and cash handed to the host is recorded on the same invoice, so the book and PayPal never disagree.

Every draw is a PayPal payout the host approves

The AI can never move money. A draw becomes a payout only when the host taps Approve.

The AI arranges, people decide

When someone needs their draw early, Banker Lady finds a swap that hurts nobody and asks, in that member's own language. Private reasons stay private.

For judges

Try it in two minutes

  1. Miss P's phone (host): tap "Joyce gave me cash". The cash is recorded on Joyce's PayPal invoice.
  2. Tap "Remind Devon, kindly". Devon gets a warm reminder in Patois, and PayPal sends its own invoice reminder.
  3. Devon's phone: tap Pay with PayPal and log in with the sandbox buyer below, or tap Cash on Devon's row in the book instead.
  4. Marcia's phone: her mother is in hospital. Send the suggested message. Banker Lady finds that Kezia can swap and still have her money 14 days before her school fees. The circle only ever sees "family emergency".
  5. Kezia's phone: tap Yes, swap. The draw stamp moves in the book and the timeline.
  6. Tap Approve payout. $300 lands in Marcia's PayPal, and the PayPal ledger tab shows the payout.

Reset demo puts everything back, and Start a new circle shows the fair draw order with a reason for every draw.
